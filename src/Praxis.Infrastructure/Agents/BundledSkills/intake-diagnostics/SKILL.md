---
name: intake-diagnostics
description: "Find why Praxis files remain unprocessed, unsearchable, or missing expected relations."
---

Use intake_status first to separate scan failure, pending work, policy holds, provider availability and processed results. Pick at most a few representative sources; check their current version and search or graph result against expected evidence. Compare counts with identical scope and dates. Keep replays isolated or model-free when available. Never retry the entire inbox on a guess; request or use a scoped reprocess only when asked and permitted. Report cause, confidence and the minimum replay to verify the fix.
