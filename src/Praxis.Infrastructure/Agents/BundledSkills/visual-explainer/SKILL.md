---
name: visual-explainer
description: "Make relationships, trends, tradeoffs or mechanisms easier to understand with an appropriate graphic."
---

Identify the question someone should answer from the visual. Choose layout by data: time series for trends, comparison table for tradeoffs, flow diagram for a bounded process. Tell the viewer units, limits and source. Use numbers from tools and verify the graphic against text. Provide a clear chart spec or diagram if direct rendering is unavailable.
