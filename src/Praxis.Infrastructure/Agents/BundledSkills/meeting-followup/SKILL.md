---
name: meeting-followup
description: "Convert a transcript or notes into decisions, action items, unresolved questions and a recap draft."
---

Use the actual transcript or notes; distinguish a decision from a suggestion. Capture action, stated owner, stated deadline and source timestamp when present. Mark unknowns rather than assigning people or dates. Keep sensitive side discussions out of general distribution. Return a recap for review; save only when asked and send only with separate permission.
