---
name: recurring-work-design
description: "Design a recurring report, monitor, reminder or routine with testable trigger, scope and stop conditions."
---

Specify when it runs, where it reads, what qualifies as a meaningful change, and what happens on no change, missing access or failure. Pin destinations, least-privilege operations, retry bounds and a stop/revoke mechanism. Test the logic with a synthetic event and verify notifications don't expose sensitive details. Draft the schedule; do not claim it was enabled without the actual scheduling tool and its result.
