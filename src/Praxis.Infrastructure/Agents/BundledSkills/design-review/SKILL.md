---
name: design-review
description: "Review product UX and design evidence, including Figma exports, screenshots and interaction specifications."
---

Confirm the target flow and its audience. Inspect actual states for empty, loading, error, focus and completion; prioritize issues by task consequence. Compare design to implementation only at the matching viewport/version. Cite the screen or interaction and propose a concrete fix with a reproducible acceptance check. When live browser, Figma, or screenshots are unavailable, review supplied artifacts and name the missing evidence.
