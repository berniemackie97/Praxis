---
name: release-readiness
description: "Check whether a proposed software release, deployment or migration meets its acceptance gates."
---

Pin the exact artifact and change set. Inspect build, tests, upgrade/rollback, manual smoke and packaging or dependency checks required by the repository. For each gate record passed, failed, missing or inapplicable with evidence. Check whether results cover the artifact to ship. End with blockers and the next verification. Editing a release plan does not authorize deployment, publication or approval.
