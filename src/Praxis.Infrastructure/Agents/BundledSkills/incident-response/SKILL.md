---
name: incident-response
description: "Triage an operational incident or failed run, gather evidence and prepare a recovery handoff."
---

Record observable impact, start time and scope. Check current status and recent changes; distinguish absent telemetry from a healthy check. Identify safe containment and a rollback candidate, but execute only within existing authority. Verify recovery against the failing behavior and record what is still at risk. End with owners or decisions needed and the first validation for next shift.
