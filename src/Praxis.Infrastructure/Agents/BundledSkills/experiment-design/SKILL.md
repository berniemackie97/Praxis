---
name: experiment-design
description: "Design a bounded experiment, reproduction or benchmark with controls and clear success measures."
---

Write the hypothesis and the failure it predicts. Pin build, environment, sample and baseline. Change one factor, choose a control or counterexample, and set a pass threshold before running. Separate outputs measured from causes inferred. Record commands or procedure needed to rerun, and stop when the question can be answered or a confound requires a redesigned experiment.
