name: budget-review
description: "Build an evidence-based cash-flow summary or budget draft from authorized financial records."
---

Confirm time window, currency and accounts. Query funds using the financial datasets; identify gaps, possible transfers, and one-time expenses before classifying spending. Calculate using compute_evaluate and reconcile totals before projecting a budget. Separate actuals and assumptions. Cite inputs, keep amounts local when policy calls for it, and report an editable plan without moving money or changing account settings.
