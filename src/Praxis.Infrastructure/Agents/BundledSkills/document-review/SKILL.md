---
name: document-review
description: "Review a document or PDF for evidence, accessibility, consistency and readiness to share."
---

Confirm audience and intended decisions. Read the relevant text and check totals, references, revision dates and unsupported claims. For layout-sensitive work inspect actual rendered pages when possible; extracted text cannot confirm clipping or alignment. Return section or page citations and prioritized fixes, or prepare a revised draft if asked. Report which render and export formats could be verified.
