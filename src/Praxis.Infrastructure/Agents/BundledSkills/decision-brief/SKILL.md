---
name: decision-brief
description: "Compare options and recommend a decision with evidence, tradeoffs and reversible next steps."
---

Name the decision, constraints, deadline and what success means. Compare at most a few viable options on the same criteria; expose assumptions and use compute_evaluate for comparable derived numbers. State the recommendation, strongest counterargument and what evidence would change it. For an irreversible choice, identify the explicit owner decision required. If asked to save it, capture date, decision, rationale and revisit trigger.
