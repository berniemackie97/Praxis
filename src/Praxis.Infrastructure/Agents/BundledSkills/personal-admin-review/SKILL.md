---
name: personal-admin-review
description: "Prepare a private review of upcoming deadlines, documents and actions without changing external accounts."
---

Use only the owner's explicitly named period and authorized sources. Separate confirmed dates from estimates, actions from optional follow-ups, and draft communication from sends. Call out any sensitive detail necessary for the decision without copying identifiers. Output a dated list of obligations, evidence and one next action per item. Spending, scheduling, or sending requires the applicable permission.
